01 / Research

From signal models
to wireless systems.

Statistical signal processing, detection and estimation, multiple-antenna technologies, and resource allocation underpin my research.

01

Cell-free & distributed massive MIMO

User-centric architectures, scalable AP–UE association, power control, and distributed processing. Current work also addresses phase misalignments and differential transmission.

Scalability · Synchronization · Resource allocation

Related papers ↗
02

Integrated sensing & communications

Signal models and detection algorithms for wireless networks that jointly serve users and sense the environment, including multi-target detection and tracking with distributed access points.

Detection & estimation · Multi-target tracking · ISAC

Related papers ↗
03

RIS & near-field processing

Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces for communications, radar sensing and localization; active surfaces, large distributed arrays, and near-field wideband beamforming.

Programmable propagation · Beamforming · Localization

Related papers ↗
04

Non-terrestrial networks & waveforms

LEO satellite macro-diversity and cell-free transmission, with OFDM and OTFS modulation. More broadly, waveform and receiver design for mobility and time-varying channels.

LEO satellites · OFDM & OTFS · Differential transmission

Related papers ↗
05

AI, optimization & edge computing

Model-based and learning-based approaches to wireless resource allocation, energy efficiency, EMF-compliant power control, and joint radio and computational resource optimization.

AI for wireless · Energy efficiency · Mobile edge computing

04 / Biography & academic service

Research, teaching
and scientific service.

I am a Full Professor at the University of Cassino and Southern Lazio, Italy, and conduct research within CNIT.

I received the M.Sc. degree in Electronic Engineering, summa cum laude, in 1994 and the Ph.D. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering in 1999, both from the University of Naples Federico II. I joined the University of Cassino in 2000 and became a Full Professor in 2018.

My research spans wireless communications and statistical signal processing, from multiuser detection and energy-efficient resource allocation to distributed MIMO, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and integrated sensing and communications.

From May 2022 to April 2026, I was also affiliated with Politecnico di Milano as a part-time Full Professor. I have held short-term research appointments at Princeton University and have experience in European research projects, international collaboration, and the supervision of doctoral and postdoctoral researchers.
